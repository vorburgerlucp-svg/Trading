## Findings fixed during this review

### R1 — HIGH — Retroactive next-bar fills after delayed data

Problem:
A strategy decision based on a delayed final bar could fill at the next chronological bar open even when that open occurred before the decision became available.

Risk:
Classic look-ahead / impossible execution.

Fix:
`isEligibleNextBar` now requires the candidate open to occur at or after the decision bar's `availableAt`.

Regression:
Delayed decision cannot fill an already-passed open; it waits for the first executable later open.

### R2 — HIGH — Scanner could reuse a previously-fresh QuantResult at a later decision time

Problem:
A QuantResult whose data-quality verdict was valid at its original `asOf` could be supplied to a later scanner run.

Risk:
Stale analysis could remain marked `usableForTrading=true`.

Fix:
Scanner snapshot `asOf` and QuantResult `asOf` must match the scanner `asOf` exactly.

### R3 — HIGH — Scanner could silently rank a partial universe

Problem:
Missing instruments were ignored, so a run could appear to represent the universe while only a subset was evaluated.

Risk:
Biased rankings and false “top candidate” conclusions.

Fix:
Scanner now records explicit coverage:
- universeMembers
- snapshotsProvided
- evaluatedInstruments
- missingInstruments
- duplicateInstruments
- complete

`rankingComplete=false` whenever coverage is incomplete.

### R4 — HIGH — Duplicate scanner snapshots could double-enter ranking

Problem:
Multiple snapshots for the same universe member were not rejected before ranking.

Fix:
Duplicate instruments are explicitly identified and excluded from ranking. Coverage becomes incomplete.

### R5 — HIGH — Market values had no explicit availability timestamp

Problem:
`lastPrice` and optional average volume were values without their own point-in-time provenance.

Risk:
A future value could be attached to an otherwise valid snapshot.

Fix:
`ScannerSnapshot` now carries:
- `lastPriceAvailableAt`
- `averageVolumeAvailableAt`

Values not available by scanner `asOf` are rejected.

### R6 — HIGH — Scanner interval identity was not enforced

Problem:
A scanner definition could theoretically rank a QuantResult from another interval.

Fix:
`QuantResult.series.interval` must match the scanner definition interval.

### R7 — HIGH — Backtest could claim corporate actions were modeled when they were not

Problem:
The caller could supply `corporateActions: modeled`, but Backtest Engine V1 does not yet adjust open positions for splits/dividends.

Risk:
An unjustified BacktestQuality grade and incorrect strategy evidence.

Fix:
Backtest Engine V1 refuses that claim until open-position corporate-action handling exists.

### R8 — HIGH — Mixed backtest series were not rejected by the engine boundary

Problem:
Bars with different interval/session/adjustment/source could reach the engine.

Fix:
Backtest now requires one uniform series and rejects duplicate bar start times.

### R9 — HIGH — Strategy parameters were not part of backtest identity

Problem:
Backtest fingerprinting used strategy `id + version` only. Different parameter sets with the same id/version could collide.

Fix:
Every BacktestStrategy now has a declarative `definition`.
NEXUS stores:
- strategyDefinition
- strategyFingerprint

The run input fingerprint includes the strategy fingerprint, and persistence checks its format and metadata checksum.

Regression:
Changing strategy parameters while keeping id/version constant changes strategyFingerprint and backtestRunId.

## Open design / implementation risks

These are intentionally NOT papered over by this review.

### O1 — HIGH before AI/live — Scanner/backtest references are not referentially verified by NEXUS Brain

Current Brain validation checks scanner/backtest ID format and puts the IDs into QuantAssessment / Decision fingerprint.

It does not yet prove that:
- the referenced run exists,
- the run belongs to the relevant instrument,
- its `asOf` is valid for the decision,
- its quality is sufficient.

Required next design:
Give NEXUS Brain read-only ScannerRunStore / BacktestRunStore access and fail closed on phantom or incompatible references.

### O2 — HIGH — Corporate actions on open positions are not implemented

Splits/dividends/mergers must be applied to portfolio positions and cash flows.

Until then the engine correctly cannot claim corporate actions are modeled.

### O3 — HIGH — Warm-up policy exists but is not yet enforced by Backtest Engine

Recursive indicators require deterministic warm-up.
No strategy order should be possible during warm-up.

### O4 — HIGH — Historical point-in-time universe ingestion is not yet real

The universe model is point-in-time capable, but a trustworthy historical membership source has not yet been ingested.

Therefore survivorship-safe historical market-wide claims are not yet proven.

### O5 — HIGH — Risk-per-trade sizing underestimates executable downside costs

Current sizing uses entry-to-stop price distance.
A production risk model must include at least:
- buy-side execution costs,
- estimated stop-side spread/slippage,
- commissions,
- gap-risk policy.

A stop is not a guaranteed loss boundary.

### O6 — MEDIUM/HIGH — Intrabar stop/TP timestamps have false precision

OHLC can prove a level was touched within a bar but often cannot prove the exact instant.

Future model should record an execution window / time precision rather than imply an exact timestamp for non-gap intrabar fills.

### O7 — MEDIUM — Equity mark-to-market convention must be explicit

Current equity curve marks an open position at bar close.

Before risk metrics are considered production grade, decide and version:
- close/mid mark-to-market, or
- estimated liquidation value including spread/commission/slippage.

Neither convention should be implicit.

### O8 — HIGH — Backtest quality still includes caller-supplied assertions

Fields such as data completeness, production-source status and point-in-time-universe status should ultimately be derived from verified stores/results, not asserted by arbitrary callers.

### O9 — MEDIUM — Strategy code changes still rely on version discipline

The declarative strategy definition captures parameter changes.
If implementation semantics change without bumping `version`, the system cannot prove that automatically.

Future option:
store a build/implementation artifact hash in the strategy identity.

### O10 — EXPECTED V1 LIMITATION — Multi-instrument portfolio engine is not complete

The reviewed executable core remains deliberately single-instrument.

Do not generalize its guarantees to a mixed-calendar portfolio until the multi-instrument availability-ordered event loop passes adversarial tests.

### O11 — EXPECTED V1 LIMITATION — Full execution lifecycle is incomplete

Still missing before realistic brokerage simulation:
- partial fills
- limit order expiry
- trading halts
- borrow/margin/short mechanics
- real bid/ask where available
